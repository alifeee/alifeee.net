I love playing games with proximity chat. It's just so fun to catch parts of conversations and to not have everyone talking over each other in a Discord channel.

I've organised a few group proximity chat games, mostly by opening a server one evening a week for a few weeks. We've played:

[Barotrauma](https://barotraumagame.com/) in 2023
: …has prox chat built in and is generally very fun. We probably averaged a submarine of 6-10 players

[Factorio](https://factorio.com/) in 2024
: I actually made a [proximity chat mod](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/proximity-voice-chat) (see the [blog post](https://blog.alifeee.net/factorio-proximity-chat/)) to play this one. It ran once a week for about 10 weeks, averaging probably 10-12 players.

[Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/) in 2025
: We used [Simple Voice Chat](https://github.com/henkelmax/simple-voice-chat) on an otherwise-Vanilla server. Again, once a week, probably an average of 10-15 players for around 3-6 months. I wrote a [blog post](https://blog.alifeee.net/2025/12/minecraft/) with some screenshots.
